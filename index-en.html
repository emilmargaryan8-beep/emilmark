ABOUT

Emil Margaryan

Born in Yerevan and based in France, Emil Margaryan writes at the intersection of languages, memories and belonging.

He studied French literature and philology in Armenia, followed by literary translation and intercultural studies in Aix-en-Provence, and now develops his writing alongside his work as a translator.

His short fiction explores memory, erasure, solitude, language and the sometimes absurd mechanisms through which human beings try to give meaning to their existence.
