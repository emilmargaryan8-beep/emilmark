The Apologies of Mr. Bernard

Emil Margaryan

🇫🇷 Français🇬🇧 English

English literary translation of the French original

Bernard Lemaire decided to become a better man on a Thursday because his dentist had cancelled.

He was fifty-one.

At twenty past ten, he found himself at home with an entire morning he had not planned for and an anaesthetic he had not, in the end, received.

He made coffee.

He opened the window.

In the street, a truck was blocking a car from pulling out of its parking space. The owner of the car was honking with admirable regularity.

Bernard closed the window.

For three months, he had been reading a book about forgiveness.

He had bought it after his divorce.

The divorce had nothing to do with forgiveness, but bookshops often put the two subjects close together.
